# Small-business pilot design

Campaign generation is constrained to existing staff, products, a phone and on-hand materials, with setup within one staff hour. No hired entertainment, custom fixtures, new inventory or deferred mailing service is assumed. Owner confirms feasibility before execution.

The brief displays one shared experiment guide. Each card has a short metric explaining what to count and how to record it. No baseline is supplied, so numeric success thresholds are not generated; a numeric metric response is replaced with baseline-first guidance. This check is a guardrail, not a comprehensive semantic guarantee.

Test one idea at a time. Hold the product, full price, audience, channel and budget equal across creative arms. Randomize exposure where practical. Record exposures, attributable purchases, revenue and costs. Compare purchase rate and contribution profit, while treating low-volume or uncertain results as inconclusive. Participation metrics are diagnostic rather than proof of profit.

Generated execution and creative interpretation remain hypotheses for owner review. Qloo verifies seed-to-entity connections, not business demand, themes, customer behavior or forecasted results. No campaign is published or paid for automatically.
